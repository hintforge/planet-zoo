Spotted Hyena (already in this corpus, see `spotted_hyena.md`) is a separate species that shipped with the base game; Striped Hyena is a distinct species added by this pack, with its own land requirement (1,162 / 1,346 m² -- the largest in this entire pack, well above Spotted Hyena's 1,162 / +92-per-adult base-game figure) and its own biome/continent profile.
