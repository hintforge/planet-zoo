## Welfare gotcha

Part of the game's ONLY multispecies exhibit: this pack's five butterfly species (Cloudless Sulphur, European Peacock, Menelaus Blue Morpho, Monarch, Old World Swallowtail) all cohabit together in one Walkthrough Exhibit -- no other Exhibit or Walkthrough Exhibit grouping in the game allows multiple species to share the same enclosure. Only Data Deficient species among the five -- unlike the other four (all Least Concern or Endangered with clear assessments), the real-world IUCN simply lacks enough data to rate this one, worth explaining if a player expects a definite status.
