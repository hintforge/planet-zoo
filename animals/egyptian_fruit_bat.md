## Welfare gotcha

This is the game's FIRST flying-creature exhibit animal, housed in an all-new walk-in structure where bats soar over the player's head as they move through -- a genuinely new exhibit category, not a reskin of the existing ground-level Walkthrough Exhibit format used by base-game and prior-DLC species. Sleeping pattern tag: Nocturnal (pre-existing base-game system, shared by all five Twilight Pack species). The pack's marketed "red-light night viewing" mechanic (for observing nocturnal species after dark) was researched and could not be confirmed to exist in the shipped game -- do not present it to a player as real; see Red Fox's entry for the fuller note.
