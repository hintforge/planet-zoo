## Welfare gotcha

Binturong is the reconciling reference for a mixing contradiction affecting three other Southeast Asia Animal Pack species: Sun Bear, Malayan Tapir, and Proboscis Monkey each show "No" in the numeric mixing tool but are listed compatible with Binturong on their own species pages -- most likely a one-directional compatibility flag (A->B recorded but not B->A) rather than genuine incompatibility.
