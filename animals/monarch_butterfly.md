## Welfare gotcha

Part of the game's ONLY multispecies exhibit: this pack's five butterfly species (Cloudless Sulphur, European Peacock, Menelaus Blue Morpho, Monarch, Old World Swallowtail) all cohabit together in one Walkthrough Exhibit -- no other Exhibit or Walkthrough Exhibit grouping in the game allows multiple species to share the same enclosure. Only Endangered species among the five (the rest are Least Concern or Data Deficient) -- the most conservation-relevant of the group and the one most likely to come up if a player asks about real-world monarch butterfly decline.
