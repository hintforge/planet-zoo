## Social, diet, enrichment

- **Social type / group:** Loose mixed herds plus bachelor groups, lekking mating system. In-game group size 3-16 (up to 1 male and 15 females; bachelor groups: males 3-6, females 3-15); easy polygynous breeder, matures at 4 years, gestation 8 months, interbirth 12 months. `[planetzoo.fandom.com 2026-10-17]`
- **Mixing compatibility:** Highland Cattle, Mute Swan, Red Deer, Tamworth Pig, Wild Boar, Wisent
- **Diet/enrichment:** Preferred food Hay, Herbivore Pellets, Fruit and Vegetables. Food enrichment: Grazing Ball Feeder, Large Barrel Feeder, Hanging Grazer Feeder, Scarecrow Feeder. Habitat enrichment: Large Ball, Large Snow Ball, Grab Ball, Skittle, Snowman Skittle Enrichment, Rubber Duck, Rubbing Pillar, Scots Pine/Tamarind Scratching Tree, Herb Scent Marker. `[planetzoo.fandom.com 2026-10-17]`
