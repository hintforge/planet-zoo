## Welfare gotcha

The game's second bat species after the Egyptian Fruit Bat, and directly confirmed as Exhibit-type housing by Frontier itself: a 1.17 patch note fixed a bug that had shown an erroneous "age of sexual sterility" Zoopedia stat for this species, with the fix note explicitly citing "there not being a sterility system for exhibits." Highly social in the wild -- solo housing risks depression/aggression per the source data, so this is not a species to keep alone even though it's exhibit-housed rather than a walkable habitat.
