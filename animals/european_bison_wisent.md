## Welfare gotcha

Elsewhere in this corpus this species may be referenced as "European Bison/Wisent" -- same animal, two common names in circulation. Its Near Threatened status is a genuine conservation good-news story: European Bison went extinct in the wild in 1927 and exist today only because of captive breeding programs, making it one of the clearest real-world stakes cases in the pack. The Eurasia Animal Pack shipped with no dedicated architectural theme (minimal new scenery), unlike most other packs.
