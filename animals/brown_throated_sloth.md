## Welfare gotcha

The game's second Exhibit/Walkthrough-Exhibit mammal, after Twilight Pack's Egyptian Fruit Bat -- and the only non-flying mammal given walkthrough-exhibit housing rather than a walkable Habitat. That choice was a point of contention in the community: a Reddit thread on Sloth housing pushed back on it, with players who wanted a full walkable habitat species instead of an exhibit animal.
