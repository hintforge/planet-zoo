## Welfare gotcha

Reported to kill and eat baby cobras. Only documented species with a per-species guest-viewing-distance data point: good <12m, neutral 12-24m, bad >24m (see `mechanics.md` Guests & Facilities). Bottom of the species-appeal scale (base ~750, alongside Common Warthog and Greater Flamingo).

## Sex ratio, practical sizing, breeding

- **Sex-ratio cap:** 4 males : 16 females. Applies as a ratio -- 4/16 = 0.25, so multiply
  the actual female count by 0.25 for the permitted male count. 2 males with 8 females is
  within the cap; 2 males with 7 females is over it and provokes sex-based fighting.
  **This refines the "one male, several females" phrasing above**, which understates the
  permitted male count: a full muster of 20 may hold 4 males, not 1.
- **Practical habitat size:** the 312 m² figure is the Zoopedia minimum **for a single
  bird**. Because peafowl need a large social group, a community recommendation past the
  opening stages is **500 m² minimum** to avoid overcrowding.
- **Breeding:** polygynous and among the easiest species in the game to breed; maturity at
  roughly 2.5 years.
- **CC sale value:** extremely low -- 10-20 at most, even with the best stats. Peafowl are a
  cash and guest-appeal play, not a Conservation Credits play.
- **Upkeep gotcha:** keeping the habitat clean is unusually hard for this species.
