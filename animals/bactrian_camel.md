## Welfare gotcha

Eats thorny/tough/bitter vegetation, carrion, even tents/rope/clothing when very hungry; can spit at guests. **In-game IUCN tag is "Domesticated,"** diverging from the real wild Bactrian camel (*C. ferus*, IUCN Critically Endangered) -- the game's entry represents the domestic form, worth flagging if a player quotes the real-world wild-camel status.
