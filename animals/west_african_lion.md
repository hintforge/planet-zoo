## Sex ratio, practical sizing, breeding

- **Sex-ratio cap:** 1 male : 29 females whenever a mate is present -- males do not tolerate
  one another under that condition, without exception. A **bachelor-only** group is the
  exception and may hold up to 4 males, or up to 30 females.
- **Practical habitat size:** the 705 m² + 111 m²/adult figures are game minimums. A
  community recommendation is **2,500 m² minimum, ideally 3,000-4,000 m²** for a pride to
  roam, sized so every lion can lie down; prides run 2-30 plus cubs. Treated as a late-game
  species for this reason.
- **Barrier:** a climb-proof fence of **at least 3m**; the cited author doubles this to 6m.
  Compare the 1m jump-clearance result in `mechanics.md` Habitats & Enclosures.
- **Breeding:** promiscuous, but reaches sterility at only 15 years -- typically the same year
  the animal dies of old age. Gestation 3 months, interbirth period 36 months, litters of
  1-3 cubs, so each female produces roughly one litter every 3 years.
- **CC sale value:** several hundred to several thousand CC through breeding, albino
  morphs highest. Compare the 1,200-1,500 CC color-morph premium noted above.
