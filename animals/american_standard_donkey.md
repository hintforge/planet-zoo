## Welfare gotcha

Each additional donkey costs nearly 19 m² -- by far the steepest per-animal land scaling of the seven Barnyard species, so a full 6-animal group gets expensive fast compared to, say, Sussex Chicken or Little Penguin-style flock animals. Eligible for the Animal Encounters petting mechanic, which shipped alongside this pack as part of the FREE Update 1.17 (also works on qualifying species from the base game and other DLCs, not exclusive to Barnyard). Mechanically it's a toggle on an existing Walkthrough Habitat requiring a guest gate, no dangerous animal present, at least one interactable species, and at least one Hygiene Station; guests leave the path to wander and interact, then want a Hygiene Station afterward or happiness drops.
