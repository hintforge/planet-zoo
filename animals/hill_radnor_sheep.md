## Welfare gotcha

Eligible for the Animal Encounters petting mechanic, which shipped alongside this pack as part of the FREE Update 1.17 (not paywalled behind Barnyard specifically -- it also works on qualifying species from the base game and other DLCs). Mechanically it's a toggle on an existing Walkthrough Habitat, requiring a guest gate, no dangerous animal present, at least one interactable species, and at least one Hygiene Station (hand-washing scenery item); guests leave the path to wander and interact, then want a Hygiene Station afterward or happiness drops. Confirmed eligible species beyond the 7 named Barnyard animals: Quokka (Oceania Pack), Llama (South America Pack), Ring-Tailed Lemur (base game) -- no comprehensive published list beyond these 10 was found **[Single source - verify on the roster beyond the 10 named]**.
