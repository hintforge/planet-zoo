## Welfare gotcha

In-game social structure (fixed harem, 1 male to 2-12 females) diverges from real-world flocking behavior, where wild flocks of 10-100 birds don't follow a fixed harem ratio -- a common in-game simplification for ratite species. Introduced alongside the pack's new career scenario "Cheetahs Never Prosper."
