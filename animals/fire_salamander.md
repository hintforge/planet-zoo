## Social, diet, enrichment

- **Social type / group:** Solitary in the wild but tolerant of others when space, mates and food allow. In-game group size 1-6; easy promiscuous breeder, matures at 5 years, gestation 3 months, interbirth 9 months. `[planetzoo.fandom.com 2026-10-17]`
- **Mixing compatibility:** none -- exhibit animals are not mixed
- **Diet/enrichment:** Preferred food: slugs, worms, insects and other invertebrates. Exhibit items: Leaf Litter A/B/C, Mister A/B/C, Broken / Narrow / Wide Hollow Log. `[planetzoo.fandom.com 2026-10-17]`
