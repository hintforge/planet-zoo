## Real-world biology (Wikipedia-sourced, not in-game data)

- **Native range:** Western Ghats, southern India (endemic)
- **Real diet:** Omnivore -- fruit, seeds, flowers, insects, small vertebrates
- **Natural social structure:** Troops of 10-20; 1-3 males (incl. alpha), many females
