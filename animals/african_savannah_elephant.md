## Welfare gotcha

Self-administers mud baths for sun/insect protection. Uses branches as tools to swat flies and scratch itself (shared trait with Indian Elephant). Top-tier species appeal (base 6750, ties with West African Lion and Western Lowland Gorilla) -- see `mechanics.md` Genetics & Breeding. Cited as high as ~1,200 Conservation Credits per release, but the 22-month gestation + 15-year maturation makes it CC-inefficient per unit time.
