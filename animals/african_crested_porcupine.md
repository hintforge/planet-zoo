## Real-world biology (Wikipedia-sourced, not in-game data)

- **Native range:** Africa; also introduced/established in Italy (Europe's only porcupine population)
- **Real diet:** Herbivore -- roots, bulbs, leaves; occasional carrion/bone-gnawing
- **Natural social structure:** Monogamous pairs, small family units
