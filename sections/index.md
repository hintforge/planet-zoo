# Sections -- index

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-08-22

For Planet Zoo, `sections/` holds **career-scenario walkthroughs** -- the closest thing this sim has to "areas." Game-mode economics (Career/Franchise/Challenge/Sandbox differences) live in `mechanics.md` Game Modes instead of here, since mode rules are systems content (`spoiler: none`), not narrative -- `sections/` is reserved for the spoiler-gated Career story surface.

## Career scenarios (12 files, one per scenario)

1. `stately_home_schooling.md` -- Goodwin House (tutorial)
2. `the_aperenticeship.md` -- Madagascar Simian Conservation Zoo
3. `bear_essentials.md` -- "Panda Park"
4. `eye_of_the_taiga.md` -- Maple Leaf Wildlife Park, Canada
5. `go_to_crater_lengths.md` -- Great Caldera Safari Park
6. `squeezing_the_margins.md` -- Myers Lake Island Zoo, Greece (Dominic Myers arc begins)
7. `power_struggle.md` -- Myers Animal Entertainment Park, India (Myers arc; "An Elephant Never Forgets" achievement)
8. `steeped_in_pollution.md` -- Myers Rainforest Conservation Project
9. `hiring_freeze.md` -- "Myers Sunshine Happiness Zoo"
10. `greener_pastures.md` -- Nevada State Eco-Zoo (Emma Goodwin introduced)
11. `bailing_out.md` -- Yamaguchi Prefecture Tranquillity Zoo, Japan ("Lin-Lin" adoption)
12. `the_last_leg.md` -- Bernie Goodwin Memorial Zoo, New York (series capstone)

Each scenario file: Location/Theme (spoiler:story), Bronze/Silver/Gold objectives (spoiler:progression), missability note.

## Aggregation targets in this folder

- `missables.md` -- cross-cutting catalog of missable objectives/one-shots, populated from `missable: yes` overlay claims across the 12 scenario files.
- `story_notes.md` -- career narrative beats across all 12 scenarios, aggregated from `vector: lore` / `spoiler: story` claims (behind the spoiler dials).
- `dlc_scenarios.md` -- **added at P3.** Lighter-touch catalog of the ~18 DLC-added Career/Timed scenarios (character, location, theme; NOT full Bronze/Silver/Gold objective breakdowns -- that granularity was outside the P3 brief's scope). See the file's own scope note.

## Where mode content actually lives

`mechanics.md` Game Modes carries the Career/Franchise/Challenge/Sandbox economic-rules table and the per-scenario difficulty note (Easy/Medium/Hard chosen per-scenario-launch) -- that content is systems-level and spoiler:none, so it does not duplicate here. `mechanics.md` DLC Layer carries the full per-pack enumeration, mechanics, and achievement-coverage findings.

## Not yet covered

Timed Scenarios side-content (statue rewards) remains out of scope. DLC-added scenarios are now covered at `dlc_scenarios.md`, but only at character/location/theme granularity, not full objective-by-objective detail -- a future pass could extend any of them if a player wants it.
