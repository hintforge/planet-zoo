# Sections -- DLC-Added Scenarios

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-08-22

**Scope note (P3):** the P3 DLC research pass captured character/location/theme detail for each DLC-added scenario, but NOT a full Bronze/Silver/Gold objective breakdown at the granularity the 12 base Career scenarios received in `sections/` at P1 -- that level of detail was outside this pass's brief. This file is a lighter-touch catalog; treat it as "what's new and who's in it," not a walkthrough. If a player wants full objective-by-objective detail for a specific DLC scenario, that's a documented gap -- flag it rather than inventing objectives.

Per `warning_tiers.md`'s guidance ("treat scenario-ending reveals with the same courtesy the puzzle dial implies"), scenarios below carrying a named recurring character or a plot outcome are tagged `puzzle-tier: 1` (name the scenario and its core challenge on request; let the player ask for the rest) -- matching the base game's 12-scenario convention. Pure build/management scenarios with no narrative payload stay `puzzle-tier: 0`.

## Americas Animal Pack

Scenario **"Cheetahs Never Prosper"** -- returning antagonist **Dominic Myers** hides out at "Casa Dom" in Mexico with his adopted cheetah cub **"Smudge,"** fending off reporters, protestors, and a wild coyote pack while trying to run an ethical zoo. **[puzzle-tier: 1]** Uses 13 habitat species (6 new to this pack + American Bison, Baird's Tapir, Cheetah, Collared Peccary, Galapagos Giant Tortoise, Pronghorn Antelope from base/other-DLC) and 7 pre-existing base-game exhibit species. Bronze/Silver/Gold objectives cover escapee capture, habitat welfare targets, protestor removal, donation boxes, inspection ratings, ticket profit, rescue-animal adoption, research levels, staff training, and a final 4.5-star inspection / 4.0-of-5 zoo rating for Gold.

_enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · spoiler: dlc:Americas Animal Pack_
