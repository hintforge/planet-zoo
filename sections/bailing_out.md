## Hard-mode community notes (Reddit sweep 2026-10-17)

- The scenario is cash-starved from the start: players pause at once and spend the loan before unpausing.
- Guests queue at closed shops for their whole visit. Players delete most shops and re-add them as vendors are hired, or move a vendor to the queue and close the shop afterwards.
- Blocking paths with staff-only path sections keeps guests in a small area while the zoo is small (free to switch back).
- Deleting an item refunds its full price, so delete-and-replace is free repair; deleting and replacing an empty habitat's door repairs its barriers.
- The habitat heat map wrongly says the peafowl cannot escape: raise the back wall.
- Since a later update (patch unknown) the Siberian Tiger can climb out of its starting habitat: delete the rocks and lower the floor, or put a tree on the rock nearest the gate.
- The scenario has butterflies enabled: one commenter farms Monarch, Blue Morpho and European Peacock in three walkthrough exhibits (auto-sell at male/female 45/45 or 40/50) for about 20k per year. Unverified, one commenter.
- Update 1.13 (official) fixed guests being unable to use the last carriage of the scenario's transport ride; one commenter still advises not opening the monorail.
- Posted under a spoiler tag by one commenter: the "place the starting animals" goals are only tracked at first placement, so animals can be moved straight back to storage if you cannot afford the habitats.
