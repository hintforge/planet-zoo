# Sections -- Missables

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-08-22

Aggregated catalog of every missable objective, one-shot scenario reward, or time-limited opportunity in the game -- the cross-cutting summary a player consults to answer "what am I about to lose if I move on?"

**Overall finding**: Career mode has **no hard fail/game-over state** -- bankruptcy does not trigger a game over, and even the "Timed Scenarios" side mechanic never locks a player out permanently. The only genuine miss-risk pattern in the base game is a small set of periodic named/rescue-animal adoption offers within four specific Career scenarios, plus one named-animal Gold-tier adoption target. Outside Career mode, nothing in the base-game systems (Franchise, Challenge, Sandbox) carries a missable-content pattern -- animals, research, and market listings are all persistent or repeatable.

## Career scenario 7 -- Power Struggle

Periodic "rescue animal" offers (part of the adopt-rescue-animals Gold objective) appear time-windowed; community reports of only receiving partial batches suggest a real (possibly buggy) miss risk. **Latest safe point:** adopt each rescue-animal offer as soon as it appears rather than waiting. This scenario is also the trigger context for the "An Elephant Never Forgets" achievement. See `sections/power_struggle.md`. [Single source -- verify - class:forum]

## Career scenario 11 -- Bailing Out

## Timed Scenarios (separate side-content bucket)

The official Frontier guide distinguishes the 12 numbered Career Scenarios above from a second bucket called **"Timed Scenarios"** (names found: *Bogged Down, Terrace Time, Before the Bern, Puddles in the Pantanal, They Grow so Fast!*), which award in-game scenery statues per medal tier.
